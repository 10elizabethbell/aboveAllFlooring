# Above All Flooring — product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`, images in `img/`), GitHub Pages
- **Mode:** Persuade

## Users
- **Primary:** homeowners in the 609 area of South Jersey thinking about carpeting a basement, family room or movie room. They find the link on Facebook (the business advertises in Burlington County and Marlton local business groups) and open it in Facebook's in-app browser on a phone. *(Area inferred from group placement and area code; not confirmed.)*
- **First reader:** the business itself. The site is a free gift to win the owner over; it must look like their own real website.

## Purpose
Turn a basement-carpet ad into a page that lets a homeowner see the work, picture their own room, and send a quote request by text in under a minute, any hour.

## Positioning
Basement carpet that makes the room the family actually uses feel finished and cozy. Their ad: "Turn Your Basement Into a Cozy Retreat with Above All Flooring!" and "There's nothing like stepping onto soft, comfy carpet in your basement!"

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone (609) 923-6269, from the ad. Whether it takes texts is unknown. No email, no booking link. Facebook page (Messenger) is their stated channel.
- Services published: basement carpet only (family room, movie-night space, cozy hangout in the ad). Anything beyond carpet is unknown.
- No prices, hours, reviews, address, years in business or owner name are published. Scott Karakashian posted the ad; his role is not stated, so the site names no person.
- Name collision: several other "Above All" businesses exist (pressure washing in Forked River, floor cleaning in Brick, out-of-state flooring). Nothing from them is used.

## Brand commitments
- Logo: black stencil "AAF" monogram over "ABOVE ALL FLOORING" (Facebook profile image). Used as a recolored mask; no colors published.
- Voice: upbeat home-makeover ("let's bring your vision to life!"), first person plural on the site ("we").

## Evidence on hand
- One work photo: a finished basement with fresh greige carpet and vacuum lines, dark navy wall, white baseboards (from the ad, 1542×2048).
- Logo (720px).
- No before/afters, no reviews.

## Principles
1. Phone first: the first screen says who, what, and how to reach them.
2. Nothing invented: no prices, reviews, towns or promises the brief doesn't support.
3. Texting and calling are the channels; the quote form hands off by text.
4. The page should feel like their room: navy wall, white baseboard, soft carpet.
